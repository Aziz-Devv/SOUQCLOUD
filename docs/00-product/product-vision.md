# Product Vision

## 1. Executive Summary

The platform is a modern, high-performance SaaS ecommerce engine designed to empower merchants to establish branded online storefronts, showcase catalogs, collect customer orders seamlessly, and fulfill orders through intuitive Dashboard and WhatsApp communication workflows.

---

## 2. Core Value Propositions

1. **Effortless Store Setup & Publishing**: Merchants can launch a customized online store within minutes using declarative, schema-driven themes and high-converting visual layouts.
2. **Frictionless Customer Order Collection**: Shoppers browse fast, edge-rendered storefronts and submit orders with minimal friction without mandatory account creation or complex payment barriers.
3. **Multi-Channel Merchant Workflows (Dashboard & WhatsApp)**: Flexible store order modes (`DASHBOARD`, `WHATSAPP`, `BOTH`) allow merchants to receive orders directly in their web dashboard or receive automated WhatsApp messages containing formatted order summaries for personalized customer communication.
4. **SaaS Subscription Model**: Merchants subscribe to the platform via a centralized, provider-abstracted subscription billing system powered by **Paddle**.

---

## 3. Product Positioning & Phase 1 Boundary

* **Phase 1 Focus**: Fast store creation, catalog management, media uploads, schema-driven visual customizer, edge storefront rendering, robust order collection (Dashboard / WhatsApp / Both modes), and merchant fulfillment management.
* **Customer Payment Boundary**: In Phase 1 and Phase 2, the platform does **not** process customer-to-merchant online payments. Merchants arrange payments independently (e.g. Cash on Delivery, Bank Transfer, in-person POS). Customer online payment gateways remain an uncommitted strategic future capability for Phase 3+.
